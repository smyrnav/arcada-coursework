# Research source notes

What was examined in each source, what it establishes, and what it cannot establish.
Screenshots referenced by evidence ID (see `evidence/evidence_register.csv`).
All captures taken 24 November 2026 with headless Firefox; PDF figures rendered from
the original publisher PDFs with pdftoppm.

## Finnish research

### PTT (2025) — *Ruuan hinta ratkaisee* (EV04, EV05)

- **Examined:** report landing page (abstract, authors, ISBN) and the full PDF; the key
  figure is Kuva 6 (PDF p. 17), consumer perceptions of private-label vs branded
  products, N = 1,197, autumn 2025.
- **Extracted:** 63% of respondents agreed PL products are clearly cheaper than branded
  products; 42% considered PL and branded quality equal; 57% agreed price decides
  between the two; 58% would rather buy a Finnish branded product than a foreign PL
  product, but if both are Finnish, 49% prefer the PL product.
- **Relevant to:** Part C — current Finnish consumer evidence to replace reliance on
  the article's 2020 US surveys.
- **Cannot establish:** actual purchasing behaviour (survey self-report), Helsinki
  specifics, or anything about our sampled products.

### KKV (2025) — *Kaupan omat merkit eli private label -tuotteet elintarvikemarkkinoilla* (EV06–EV09)

- **Examined:** landing page and full PDF. Key figures: Kuvio 1 (p. 19, PL share of
  grocery sales 2016–2023, ~25–26% and stable), Kuvio 4 (p. 22, assortment growth in
  EAN counts), Kuvio 5 (p. 24, PL vs non-PL selling prices).
- **Extracted:** PL share of Finnish grocery sales value stayed around a quarter over
  2016–2023; assortment grew; PL prices are generally lower than non-PL prices in the
  same categories. The dataset identifies 150+ Lidl private-label brands.
- **Relevant to:** Part A (2.3, Lidl brand count) and Part C (market context).
- **Cannot establish:** the market after 2023 — the data ends in 2023, so it must not
  be quoted as a 2026 figure. Also nothing about individual product prices we sampled.

## International research

### NielsenIQ (2025) — *Finding Harmony on the Shelf* (EV10)

- **Examined:** the official press release page (the full report is gated).
- **Extracted:** survey of 17,000+ consumers in 25 countries (Dec 2024–Jan 2025); 53%
  of global respondents increasingly buy private label; PL global sales growth +4.3%
  year-on-year. Percentage details quoted by secondary sources conflict (68–72%
  "good alternative"), so the report text avoids those specific numbers.
- **Cannot establish:** Finland-specific attitudes.

### PLMA (2026) — *A mosaic of markets* (EV11)

- **Examined:** the article page including the European value-share map.
- **Extracted:** private label reached 38.8% value share across 17 monitored European
  markets in 2025 (EUR 387bn, +4.1% vs +2.7% for manufacturer brands). Country shares
  range from 50%+ (Switzerland) to 23.6% (Norway); Finland is not separately identified
  in the visible content, so no Finland claim is made from this source.
- **Cannot establish:** Finland's specific position.

## McKinsey article (EV01–EV03)

- **Examined:** the lecturer-supplied PDF. Pages rendered: Exhibit 1 (p. 2, brand
  switching), Exhibit 2 (p. 3, purchase intent), and the three-stage maturity model
  (p. 5).
- **Use in the report:** analytical framework only. Its survey figures are US,
  March–September 2020, and are never presented as current or Finnish.

## Retailers (EV12–EV19)

- **K-Ruoka (EV12):** the snack listing filtered to Pirkka via the own-brand filter.
  Dataset values confirmed on screen (Pirkka Original 275 g, €2.19, €7.96/kg).
  **Caveat visible in the capture:** the selected store is K-Citymarket Iso Omena
  (Espoo), which confirms the report's location-confidence limitation.
- **S-kaupat (EV13):** crisp category page 1 of 4 (93 products). Dataset values
  confirmed: Xtra €1.71 (€6.84/kg), Coop maalaisperunalastut €1.65 (€9.43/kg), Taffel
  Sips 260 g €2.65 (€10.19/kg). Private labels and national brands appear interleaved
  in one grid — the digital-merchandising observation in section 2.5.
- **Lidl (EV14–EV15):** both product URLs from the preliminary dataset now return
  "Not found". This is recorded as an access failure, not worked around: the Lidl rows
  remain low-confidence, older captures.
- **Wolt venues (EV16–EV17):** venue pages for the central (Rautatieasema) and
  suburban-proxy (Vuosaari) kiosks; these are the menus the Part B dataset was sampled
  from. Prices are delivery-platform prices, not in-store shelf prices.
- **R-kioski info pages (EV18–EV19):** official addresses and opening hours for both
  locations.

## Method note

Screenshots show real rendered pages; nothing was regenerated, redrawn or edited.
Full-page captures keep surrounding context (publisher, date, captions) so each figure
is identifiable and verifiable against the URL in the register.
